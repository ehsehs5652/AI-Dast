# HackerOne

Use a descriptive title, asset and weakness. Organize the body as summary,
recorded reproduction steps, impact, and recommended fix. Supporting materials
are references to validated artifacts. A severity or CVSS proposal is optional;
leave it null without supporting data. Programs may require their own template
and additional fields, which this generic local draft does not supply.

[HackerOne submitting reports](https://docs.hackerone.com/en/articles/8473994-submitting-reports).
